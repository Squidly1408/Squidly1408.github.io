Privacy Policy

Furni4 — Last updated September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Furni4, a room and office layout planner, collects, how it's used, and your choices. Furni4 is an independent project operated by Lucas Newman (robotduck961@gmail.com). This is a plain-language policy for a small independent project, not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

By default, none. Your room layouts and projects are saved automatically to your own browser's local storage and never leave your device.

If you use the optional Share feature, the specific layout you choose to share — its floor plan geometry, furniture, and any floorplan reference image it includes — is stored on a small server-side store so the share link works. No account or personal information is required to use this feature.

Third-party services

Furni4 doesn't use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. The only server-side component is:

  • Share-link storage — only the layout data you explicitly choose to share, so a link can be generated.

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate, maintain, and improve Furni4 — for example, to generate and serve a share link you've created. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data (including a shared link you'd like taken down) by contacting us at robotduck961@gmail.com. Since projects live in your own browser, you can also delete them yourself at any time from the Projects menu.

Data retention & security

We keep shared-link data only as long as needed for the purpose above. We take reasonable steps to protect data but, as with any online service, we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Furni4 is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of Furni4 after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email robotduck961@gmail.com.
