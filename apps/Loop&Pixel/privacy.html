Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Your photos are turned into charts on your own device. They are never uploaded.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.
  • Anything you save stays in your browser’s local storage, and you can delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Loop & Pixel (“we”, “us”) is a free web app that converts photos into knitting and crochet charts and generates sized patterns. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Questions can be sent to [contact email].

2. Photos you add

When you choose or drop a photo, it is read and processed entirely inside your web browser using standard browser features (the Canvas API). The image is resized, its colours are analysed and a chart is produced, all locally. Your photo is not sent to us or to anyone else. We never see it, and the original image is not stored: it is discarded when you close or reload the page.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your work between visits, the app saves a small amount of data in your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

WhatWhy
Settings (craft, crochet terms, chart options)So the app opens the way you left it
Your current chart (a grid of colour numbers and a palette, not the photo)So you can keep working on it later
Saved charts and projects (“My makes”)So you can return to them
Step progress and row countersSo ticked-off steps stay ticked

You can delete individual items from My makes, or remove everything by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. If you use a private/incognito window, the data is removed when the window closes.

4. Offline support

The app uses a service worker to store its own files (code, styles, fonts and icons) in your browser’s cache so that it loads quickly and works without an internet connection. This cache contains only the app itself, never your photos or personal information.

5. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or password is required, and none is collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or third-party tracking scripts are used.
  • No cookies are set by the app.
  • Fonts are bundled with the app rather than loaded from a third-party font service.

6. Hosting and server logs

The app’s files are delivered by a web hosting provider. Like almost every website, the host may automatically record standard technical information when your browser requests a file, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. These logs are used only to keep the service secure and running, are kept for a limited period under the host’s own policies, and are not combined with anything you do inside the app. Hosting provider: [hosting provider and link to its privacy policy].

7. Copy to clipboard

When you tap “Copy”, the pattern or chart text is placed on your device’s clipboard by your browser. We don’t read your clipboard.

8. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal information is collected, it can be used by makers of all ages. Parents and guardians may want to supervise younger children’s use of the internet in general.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act or the CCPA to access, correct or delete personal information. Because Loop & Pixel does not collect or store personal information on any server, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete. All of your data is under your control on your device. If you have a question, contact us at the address above.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes (for example, if an optional feature that needs a server is ever added), we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
