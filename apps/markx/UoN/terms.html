What markx is

markx is a free, independent tool that helps you estimate where you stand in a course and what marks you might need on the rest of your assessments and how your GPA might change.

Estimates, not official results

Everything markx shows is an estimate based only on what you enter. It doesn't know about things like scaling, moderation, rounding rules, late penalties, special consideration or supplementary assessment. Always check your Course Outline, your course coordinator and your official results on myHub. Don't rely on markx alone for decisions about your study.
