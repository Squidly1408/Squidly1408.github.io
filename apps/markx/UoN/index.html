Questions

Where do I find my assessment weightings?

In your Course Outline, under the assessment section. Each item lists its weighting as a percentage, and any minimum mark you need to reach. Course Outlines are linked from your course's Canvas site and the UoN course handbook.

What's a minimum mark (hurdle)?

Some assessments say you must reach a set mark on that item to pass the course, no matter how well you do overall. A final exam with a 40% minimum is common. markx makes sure every "What you need" figure also clears those minimums, and warns you if a marked item missed one.

How is "what you need" worked out?

markx adds up the course marks you've already earned, then finds the lowest average across everything not yet marked that gets you to your goal grade. If an item has a minimum mark higher than that average, it needs at least the minimum and the others can be a little lower.

My weights don't add to 100%. What happens?

Results are scaled as if they did, so the maths still works while you fill things in. Double-check against your Course Outline though, since a missing item will throw out your numbers.

How is my GPA calculated?

UoN uses a 7-point scale: HD is 7, D is 6, C is 5, P is 4 and FF is 0. Each grade is multiplied by the course's unit value, added up, then divided by the total units. Ungraded passes (UP) don't count. markx shows it to one decimal place. Your official GPA is the one on myHub.

What if I miss a minimum mark or fail a course?

You may be eligible for a supplementary assessment or other options. Talk to your course coordinator early, and check the UoN policies on assessment and adverse circumstances. markx can't tell you what you're eligible for.

Is my data saved? Can I use it on my phone too?

It saves automatically in this browser on this device. To move it, open "Back up or move your data" below, download a backup file (or copy the text), then load it on your other device.
