Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version: markx doesn't collect, store, share or sell your personal information. Everything you enter stays in your browser, on your device.

What markx stores and where

The courses, assessments, weights, marks, goals and past results you enter are saved in your browser's local storage on your device, so they're still there next time you visit. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Anyone who can use your device and browser profile can see this data. To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Backups

If you download a backup file or copy a backup, markx creates it on your device. Where you save or paste it is up to you, and it's then covered by the privacy terms of wherever you put it.

What markx doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No forms that send your information anywhere.

Hosting

markx is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the page, to run and secure its service. markx can't see these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts

markx UoN loads its fonts from the same site it's hosted on, so your browser doesn't contact any font service or other third party when you use it.

The University of Newcastle

markx is not connected to the University of Newcastle, Australia. It doesn't connect to myHub, Canvas or any other university system, and the university receives no information from markx.

Links to other sites

markx links to other sites, such as GitHub. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave markx.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub. Please don't include personal details there, since issues are public.

Disclaimer: markx is an independent, student-built project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to the University of Newcastle, Australia. "University of Newcastle" and "UoN" are used only to describe who this tool is for, and no university logos are used. Results are estimates, not official grades.
